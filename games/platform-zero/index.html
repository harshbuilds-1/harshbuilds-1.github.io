How to play

Read each clue and press its matching shape. Use your mouse, touch, or these keys:

1 Circle   2 Triangle   3 Square

  1. Find the earliest departure in the Hall.
  2. After POWER RESTORED, press Circle again to travel to Control.
  3. Choose the first stop after START. Press Circle after confirmation to travel to the Platform.
  4. Choose the final destination. After the train arrives, press Circle to board.

A wrong Hall or Control answer stays in the same puzzle. A wrong Platform answer opens frame 6; Circle retries. The raised Circle is also the continue or boarding control. There is no time limit.

This is a separate browser demonstration of the eight-frame design. It does not test the ShapesXR headset build. Room travel here switches the browser scene.
