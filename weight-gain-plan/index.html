Your first 12 weeks

Test, start, check, retest

  1. Week 0

    Get your blood tests

    They rule out a medical reason for staying thin and give a kidney baseline. Enter the results in the health check above. Pregnant, planning a pregnancy or breastfeeding? Ask your doctor before any supplement.

    CBC anaemiaTSH thyroidHbA1c sugarKFT kidneysUrine ACR kidneysLFT liverVit D · B12 · ferritin
  2. Week 1

    Start gently

    Drink half the shake each day. Begin strength training 3 times a week: squats, rows, push-ups and light dumbbells.

  3. Week 2

    Full routine, weekly check-in

    Full shake every day. Once a week, weigh yourself on the same morning before eating, and measure your waist at the navel.

  4. Week 3 onward

    Adjust

    Weight not moving? Add 150–200 kcal a day, such as a roti with 1 tsp ghee or a katori of curd with a banana. Waist growing fast while arms and legs don't change? Cut sweets and use fewer dates.

  5. Week 12

    Retest

    Repeat the kidney test, urine ACR and liver test, and compare with week 0. At 1–2 kg a month, you should be about 3–6 kg up.

Before you pay

  • Buy from the brand's own website or a large authorised seller, not Instagram or small marketplace sellers.
  • The label shows an FSSAI licence number, batch number and expiry. Scan the authenticity code if there is one.
  • Look up the batch on trustified.in. Results apply only to the batch tested.
  • Whey is the first ingredient. Glycine, taurine or glutamine near the top can mean the protein count is inflated.
  • Under about 10 g added sugar per serving, with no "proprietary blend" and no herbal extracts.

Warning signs

Know when to stop

Four levels, from normal changes to an emergency. Match what you feel to a level and do what it says.

  1. Level 1

    Normal: keep going

    • Hungrier than usual
    • Mild bloating in week 1
    • Sore muscles for a day or two after training
    • Gaining 0.25–0.5 kg a week
  2. Level 2

    Watch: adjust the plan

    • Bloating or gas past 2 weeks: halve the shake or switch to whey isolate
    • Acne flare or loose stools
    • Waist growing faster than arms and legs
    • Gaining over 1 kg a week for 2 weeks: cut sweets and dates
  3. Level 3

    Act: see a doctor within days

    • Swollen feet, face or eyes
    • Foamy or very dark urine
    • Blood pressure 140/90 or higher, twice
    • Round, puffy face or new stretch marks after fast gain (hidden steroid sign)
    • Racing heart, shaky hands or weight still falling (thyroid)
  4. Level 4

    Urgent: get help now

    • Chest pain or severe breathlessness
    • Fainting
    • Swollen lips, tongue or throat, or hives after a product
    • Very little or no urine
    • Blood pressure 180/110 or higher with headache or blurred vision

Never take these

  • Ayurvedic or herbal weight-gain powders, capsules and syrupsEspecially ones promising "5 kg in 15 days". Weight-gain products like these have been found with hidden steroids (dexamethasone) and appetite drugs (cyproheptadine).

  • Appetite-booster syrups or tabletsOnly if a doctor prescribes them.

  • Unbranded or very cheap gainersUnknown sellers and unbranded tubs have no batch testing you can check.
